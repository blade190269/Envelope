A **tattered letter** is a [letter](@envelope:letter) that has been damaged and appears torn and faded.\
Can be sealed, mailed or placed on a wall.\
Copying it with a [letter and quill](@envelope:letter_and_quill) will produce a regular [letter](@envelope:letter).

## Obtaining
Tattering occurs when a [fox](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fox) spits out a letter from its [mouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mouth).

## Usage
![Tattered Letter interface](@envelope:gui/tattered_letter){width=370 right}

Usage of tattered letter is mostly identical to a regular [letter](@envelope:letter).
