**Soulbound seal stamp** is a [seal stamp](@envelope:seal_stamp) that creates soulbound seals.\
All seals created with soulbound seal stamp will have a **Sculk** material.

## Obtaining
Soulbound seal stamp can be crafted by combining an [echo shard](@minecraft:echo_shard) with any regular [seal stamps](@envelope:seal_stamp).

## Soulbound Seal
![Soulbound Seal in the tooltip](@envelope:gui/soulbound_seal){width=315 right}

Soulbound seal is a special type of seal, that **prevents items** sealed with it **from dropping** from sealer's inventory **when they die**.\
If the item, sealed with soulbound seal, is given to **another player**, they **will be unable to break the seal until the sealer dies**.

When the player who created a soulbound seal dies, **all soulbound seals they created will be "unlocked"** and will behave like a regular seal.\
This will be reflected in the tooltip as well, with lock and sculk tentacles disappearing. Sculk seal itself will remain, however.

> [!NOTE]
> Unlike regular seal stamp, soulbound seal stamp has 8 durability, and when fully used will convert back to its original seal stamp.
