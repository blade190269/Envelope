**Mail** is an item that's being carried by a courier during [delivery](@envelope:delivery).\
Delivered mail will show a sender [address](address) in item's tooltip.

## Mailable
To apply an [Address Tag](@envelope:address_tag) to the item or insert the item into
[Mailbox](@envelope:mailbox) sending slot, it needs to have a `#envelope:mailable` tag.

## Delivery Log
![Delivery Log](@envelope:misc/delivery_log_tooltip){width=426 right}

Delivery log has information about sender and recipient addresses, as well as other information related to the delivery: payback status, return reason, etc.

When a mail was delivered, holding `Shift` will show a delivery log of this item, instead of a sender's address.

> [!NOTE]
> Delivery Log is also shown in the inbox, when hovered over an address.
